「持續不斷的溫暖陪伴，文藻USR國際志工頒獎典禮!」

2021年10月10日

「持續不斷的溫暖陪伴，文藻USR國際志工頒獎典禮!」 (撰寫人:黃湘嫈、季慕華、周則銘)

[2021/09/28高雄黃湘嫈報導]文藻USR小螺絲釘計畫，九月二十八日於校內求真樓301教室，舉辦柬埔寨國際志工頒獎大會，感謝文藻小螺絲釘們這一期共12位國際志工，不斷持續為柬埔寨孩子們的無償付出，以長期陪伴取代短期支援，陪伴柬埔寨孩子們成長，期盼能成為他們生命中有影響力的過客。文藻USR的小螺絲釘們服務柬埔寨當地偏鄉孩童，迄今已3個年頭，即使今年因疫情因素，小螺絲釘們無法如願前往當地陪伴孩童，但仍以線上教學方式持續幫助柬埔寨孩童學習成長，期待為孩子們帶來實質的進步，借助小小螺絲釘的力量推進該村莊的兒童教育。

正值午餐時間，助理林思如帶來好吃的便當溫心暖腹，讓大家活力充沛。典禮開始，由孫韵芳老師代表團隊感恩致詞，並和翁一珍老師分別頒授國際志工感謝狀，鼓勵每位願意在台灣線上教學的國際志工們，並致贈每一位國際志工一份手工禮物，內容包含文藻USR小螺絲釘計畫杯墊以及手工肥皂，孫老師親手製作的心意讓每位志工都覺得溫暖無比。翁一珍老師也帶來了美味的零食分享給大家，氣氛和樂融融。頒獎後，孫韵芳老師再次提醒柬埔寨學童英文課程內容以及分配工作，期待國際志工打造更好的學習品質與效果。

大會結束前，與會老師們向每位國際志工們精神喊話，感謝他們願意踏出舒適圈，期待一同前往柬埔寨陪伴偏鄉孩童們成長，必定能獲得和別人不一樣的經歷與回憶，累積更多教學經驗。孫韵芳老師同時以最溫柔的話語歡迎新血加入，共創更多美好的故事，永續志工服務。倘若疫情穩定減緩而無慮，文藻小螺絲釘們期盼於明年八月能再次前往柬埔寨，陪伴偏鄉孩童們學習。

The Wenzao USR WHC held the Cambodia International Volunteer Award Ceremony on September 28th in Q301 of Qiuzhen Building. The event is to thank the Wenzao USR WHC for their continuous free contribution to the Cambodian children, their long-term companionship and support of the Cambodian children in order to help them succceed in their lives. The Wenzao USR WHC has gone to Cambodia to serve local children in rural areas for six years. Even though the USR WHC were unable to visit and accompany the children due to the epidemic this year, they still continued to help Cambodian children to learn and grow through online teaching. Everyone look forward to bringing substantial progress to the children, and they also hope that their tiny efforts can help advance the country.

Teaching assistant Jollis Lin brought delicious lunchboxes to the event so that everyone can have an energetic meeting. Professor Janny Sun, the representative of the school, thanked and encouraged every international volunteer for their willingness to teach on stage or online, and give each international volunteer a handmade gift bag. The gift bag contains the Wenzao USR project cup and handmade soaps personally made by professor Janny Sun, making every volunteer feel warm. Professor Janet Weng also brought delicious snacks to share with everyone, and the atmosphere was pleasant. Later, professor Janny Sun explained the course content and assigned work during the meeting. The international volunteers look forward to creating better learning quality and more positive impact on Cambodian children.

At the end of the meeting, the teachers also cheered to every international volunteer, thanking them for their willingness to step out of their comfort zone and help the Cambodian children in remote villages to grow. They will surely get different experiences and memories from each other. Professor Janny Sun also welcomes everyone with the most tender words and looks forward to creating many beautiful stories together. If the epidemic slows down and stabilizes, Wenzao USR WHC plans to travel to Cambodia again in August next year to visit the children in remote areas.
