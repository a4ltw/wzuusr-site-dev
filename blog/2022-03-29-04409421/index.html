你也想為這個社會盡一份心力嗎?

2022年3月29日

你也想為這個社會盡一份心力嗎? （撰文：潘怡均、曾若嘉、游浚承、王品皓 ; 翻譯: 季慕華）

(2022/3/26，高雄) 一群文藻外語大學的學生人共41人，一早便搭車或騎車，克服交通狀況順利來到義大癌醫院6樓，與13位其他有志參與志工的義大學生和社會人士，一起參加全日的新進志工訓練。自2019以來，溫暖白色巨塔的小螺絲釘和義大醫院合作，為深碗和微型課同學提供紮實的志工訓練。義大醫院邀請了醫院護理師以及資深志工，為現場的聽眾分享醫療相關知識、醫院的術語，以及擔任志工多年來的經驗談等等。此次的活動除了講座以外，另外還安排由志工帶領學生參觀整座醫院，讓學生更進一步熟悉整個義大的醫療環境，例如門診服務和分流。 本次內容方面，特別有與生死相關的內容，不少學生在演講的過程中感動得流下眼淚，並表示他們非常期待正式擔志工後的體驗。許多學生在最後也分享了一整天下來的感想。 目前正就讀文藻外語五專部的女同學表示，很開心可以參加此次的志工訓練，參觀服務場域後才發現原來醫學還分這麼多細項。本來對於醫院沒有什麼太大的了解，經過志工們的講解後，對於義大醫院也有了基本的認識；同時也對志工們大眾服務且不求回報的態度，深感敬佩和感激。同學期待正式排班輪值之後，可以實際服務，累積一些工作經驗，這不止會留下深刻的回憶，更是大開眼界的機會! 其他一同前來的同學也紛紛有感而發，感謝文藻開設了這門微型課程使學生有機會參與醫院的志工工作，模擬各種情況，體會醫療人員和志工的辛苦，瞭解到了原來醫院內有很多幕後默默付出的人，他們的辛苦非一般肉眼可見的，學生們因此更加感恩與知足，如有機會付出，也會盡最大的力量去幫助他人！ 最後有兩名文藻外語大學部三年級的學生激動的表示，之前對於志工活動完全不感興趣，但是這次志工演講和訓練，讓他們對於志工活動完全改觀，護理師及志工們毫無保留的指導和付出，令其中一位同學感動得流下男兒淚，非常期待即將展開的志工的體驗! 新進志工訓練的結束，是另一個里程的開始；穿上志工服代表承諾也是使命，不論義大醫院或文藻的師長更樂見志工服務能夠永續，為社會福祉注入更大的活力。

Do you also want to give back? (Written by Yi-chun Pan, Jon-chia Tseng. Jun-chun Yo, Ping-hao Wang; Translated by Yolanda Chi）

(2022/3/26, Kaohsiung) A group of 41 Wenzaorians, either by bus or on their motorbikes and scooters, overcame the difficulties in traffic they had encountered and managed to arrive at the venue for the whole-day new volunteer training on the 6th floor of E-Da Cancer Hospital, together with 13 other participants from E-Da University and people from various walks of life. Ever since 2019, WHC-USR has been collaborating with E-Da Hospital to provide a solid volunteer training for the USR “deep-bowl” and micro courses. On E-Da side, nursing professionals and senior volunteers were invited to share related medical knowledge, common medical terms, and years of experience of volunteering. In addition to lectures, a tour around the hospital guided by senior volunteers was arranged to help participants understand better the medical environment such as outpatient services and triage. In term of the content, one special topic covered life and death and moved numerous participants to tears, who looked forward to being engaged in the service as qualified volunteers more than ever. At the end of the one-day training, a number of students shared their reflection: A female student who currently studies at Wenzao’s junior college stated that she was glad to be able to attend the training. The tour on the site helped her discovered the different areas in medical science, which she formerly had little understanding of but now acquired basic comprehension after senior volunteers’ illustration. She was also impressed by and showed gratitude to senior volunteers for their selfless devotion. Students early waited for the day to serve on the arranged schedule and to accumulate work experience—a great memory and a perfect chance for an eye-opening experience. The other students also expressed their opinions respectively, showing their gratitude to Wenzao for offering a micro course for students to participate in medical volunteering, and simulating all possible situations to experience the efforts made by medical personnel and volunteers. It was not until they came to the actual site in the hospital that they witnessed so many nameless heroes who quietly made their dedication. As a result, students learned to feel satisfied and appreciate for what they had been given, and would seize the chance to give back with all their might and efforts.
Finally, two Wenzao undergraduates of the junior years mentioned in agitation that they had not the slightest idea or interest in volunteering but the lectures and training had totally changed their perspective on voluntary work. The unreserved guidance and offer of help made by the nursing professionals and senior volunteers even jerked the tears out of a male student, raising the morale among the participants for volunteering.
The end of the new volunteer training is the beginning of another milestone. Putting on the volunteer vest means a commitment and a mission. Either E-Da Hospital personnel or Wenzao faculty will gladly see the sustainable development of volunteering and the vitality in bringing about social welfare.
