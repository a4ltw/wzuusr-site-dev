小螺絲釘變標準病人了

2022年11月30日

小螺絲釘變標準病人了 (撰文：周則銘)

2022年11月19號，文藻小螺絲釘完成了他們在高雄榮民總醫院的標準病人培訓課程。培訓課程內容包含OSCE的介紹，包括模擬教室及考場在內的設施導覽，如何準備劇本，以及模擬訓練。

透過小組討論，六個來自高雄榮民總醫院的標準病人訓練員教導學生們，要成為一個標準病人他們應該要知道的事。訓練員們向學生們解釋劇本, 需要牢記的重要資訊,以及他們在OSCE考試可能會遇到的事。最重要的，訓練員們向學生展示了要如何演繹病人的角色。訓練員和學生們之間的互動非常良好，而這對所有人來說，都是一個難能可貴的經驗。

在短暫的休息過後，訓練員及學生們前往OSCE測驗中心進行他們的模擬練習。練習中，每個人輪流扮演醫生及病人的角色。這個模擬練習讓標準病人有機會先體驗OSCE考試的樣子，而這是在大學教室裡沒辦法得到的可貴經驗。大家在扮演不同角色上都獲得了許多樂趣。

在大家都順利的完成OSCE的模擬訓練後，就到了大家分享當日體驗的時間。所有的訓練員都稱讚文藻的學生非常聰明，不像以往他們訓練的退休人員或較年長的專業人士，學生們總能夠快速的學習並接收新知識。訓練員們也提到有些學生擁有表演的天賦，他們能夠將劇本中的角色真實地塑造出來。文藻的學生們則說他們從訓練員身上學到許多，並認為這次的活動是個難得的經驗。「要成為一個標準病人將涉及到演繹及醫療知識，而這些是你在文藻的課堂上沒辦法學到的。」林清煌醫師在結語時說道，他很感動所有人在這次的體驗上學到很多，並謝謝大家這次的參與。

2022 SP training at Kaohsiung Veterans Hospital By Peter Tze-Ming Chou

On November 19, 2022, students of WHC-USR completed their SP training at Kaohsiung Veterans Hospital. The training included an introduction to OSCE, tour of facility including the simulation classroom and testing classrooms, how to prepare the script, and simulation practice.

Through small group instruction, six SP Trainers from Kaohsiung Veterans Hospital came to teach the students what they need to know about being a SP. The trainers showed the students how to interpret the script, key information to remember, and what they may encounter during OSCE exam. Most importantly, the trainers showed the students how to act out the patient’s role. There were lots of interactions between the students and their trainers, it was a rewarding experience for everyone.

After a short break, the students and trainers head into the OSCE examination facility for their simulation practice. Everyone took turns playing the role of the patient and the doctor. The simulation is a chance to give the SP a chance to experience what is like during the OSCE exams. This is a unique experience that you would not find in the university classroom. Everyone had fun acting out the different roles.

After everyone has successfully completed the OSCE simulation, it was time for sharing of the day’s experience. All the trainers said that Wenzao’s students were all very smart, they were able to understand and learn the information very quickly unlike the retirees or older professionals who they usually train. The trainers also mentioned how some of the students have a talent in acting, they were able to realistically portrait their role in the script.

The Wenzao students also said that this activity is very rewarding and they all have learned a lot from their trainers. “Being an SP involves acting and medical knowledge, this is something you cannot learn from the classes in Wenzao” said one student. Dr. Ching-Huang Lin, Director of Medical Education and Research at Kaohsiung Veterans Hospital, concluded the by saying that he was very touched to hear how everyone gain so much from the experience and thanked everyone for their participation and efforts.
