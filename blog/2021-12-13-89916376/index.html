12/24小螺絲釘USR教師社群：善導書院-蝸牛媽媽的故事

我們和別人的距離有多遠？是一公尺？還是十公里？還是地球到月球的距離？大多情況，這些距離是没有意義的。人生真正有意義的量測，是以「愛」來衡定我們的距離。如果彼此有愛，那千山萬水都不會困難！但如果没有了愛，咫尺亦如天涯。有一位單親媽媽，曾自己孤單的養育著孩子，但因不忍看見其他有同樣境遇的婦女為了生活如此艱辛，便發起「易子而教」，愛使寒冷的偏鄉成了溫暖的樂園，這便是善導書院最初的由來。

善導書院陳文靜院長在八八風災時，來到了高樹協助救災，發現偏鄉問題嚴重，便毅然決然全人投入。如今一轉瞬，二十四個年頭過去了，陳院長陪伴無數孩童成長，雖然過程艱辛，但她從不輕言放棄任何一個孩子。陳院長常說自己是個蝸牛媽媽，背著這些孩子，慢慢的往前爬，期許未來每個孩子都能夠在書院的教育下，學習一技之長，翻轉自己的人生，也成為能夠用自己的力量去幫助更多需要幫助的人。12/24的教師社群，我們邀請到陳院長來分享蝸牛媽媽的故事一步一腳印的歷程。歡迎大家一起來參加！也為蝸牛媽媽打氣！ WHC-USR教師社群饗宴 🌈日期/時間: 12/24（五）12:00-14:00 🌈地點: Z1211 🌈講者：慈惠善導書院 陳文靜院長 🌈題目：善導書院-蝸牛媽媽的故事

12/24 WHC-USR Teachers’ Community: The Sundoor School—The Story of Mother Snail

How far are we from other people? One meter? Or 10 kilometers? Or the distance from the Earth to the Moon? In most cases, measuring the distance is meaningless. The significant measurement in life is actually that of love and care, which determines our distance. If we care about each other, then a thousand miles does not matter at all. Yet, without love and care, a distance, though short as only one yard away, would still seem remote as the distance to the world’s end. Once upon a time, there was a mother in the status of a single-parent, who raised her children all by herself. Out of empathy for women in a tough situation similar to hers, she launched a mode of education, namely to “exchange children with others for teaching purposes”; as a result, love and care have melted the freezing distance between people, turning a remote rural area into a heart-warming paradise. This is the origin of the Sundoor School.

When Dean Wen-ching Chen of the Sundoor School first came to Gaushu Township to assist in a relief operation after Typhoon Morakot in 2009, she found the problems in the rural areas to be so severe that she determined to stay and indulge in solving the problems. Twenty-four years has passed since her earliest attempt and Dean Chen has accompanied numerous children in their growth. Frequented by hardship throughout the process, she has never given up any of the children. Dean Chen often calls herself “Mother Snail”, in the image of a mother carrying those kids on her back and crawling slowing forward. She expects every child to learn professional skills to flip into success and to help more people in need with their acquired strengths.

For our Teachers’ Community gathering on Dec. 24, we have invited Dean Wen-ching Chen to share the story of Mother Snail, showing the struggles and footsteps of the Sundoor School. The physical distance between people is never a problem, and mutual care blossoms into possibilities for Sundoor. You are welcome to join us and let’s give a bid hand to Mother Snail.
