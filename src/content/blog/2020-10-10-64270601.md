今年12位國際志工on board了！
她們即將擔任柬埔寨KKS國小的遠距課程英文小老師！分享我們這學期其中一位國際志工的參加動機，讓老師們看了真的非常的感動！
...........
今年寒假到柬埔寨暹粒當國際志工，有到村落小學帶活動、在村落蓋房子和帶小朋友去吳哥窟寫生。因為這六天的經驗讓我深深愛上柬埔寨，也讓自己對於國際志工這塊產生很多想法與渴望，無論在其中是參與什麼活動或以什麼方式付出。

今年四月在準備轉學的資料時，有在網路上看到USR計畫的相關資訊與去年在柬衛教的影片，超級感動⋯會一直想到自己寒假的親身感受，所以就下定決心若真的轉到文藻後，一定要加入這個柬埔寨國際醫療志工團！！算是最渴望做的事吧！❤️🇰🇭

未來想到偏鄉當英文老師。知道自己還有很多很多不足的能力，期許可以在接下來一年的時間，不管是透過遠距教學還是平常的課程，都可以不斷吸收和進步！在實際到達柬的小學前，會更努力充實自己，教學經驗和表達能力都是！！😊

There are totally 12 international volunteers on board now. They will be responsible for being as on line English teacher for KKS school in Cambodia in the following few months. We would like to share a participating motivation from one of our international volunteers. Teachers were really touched after seeing this. 

I served as an international volunteer in Siem Reap, Cambodia on this winter vacation. I went to an elementary school in the village and held some activities for them. Moreover, I also built houses and took those kids to Angkor Wat to sketch. Because of these six days, I was deeply in love with Cambodia which also brought me more thoughts and desires of being an international volunteer no matter what activities or roles I am playing. 

While I was preparing the transfer test in April, I saw the information of USR Program and the video shot in Cambodia from internet. I was extremely touched and kept recalling the memory I had experienced on this winter vacation. 
I had made up my mind if I luckily transferred to Wenzao, I would definitely join this international medical volunteering team in Cambodia. This is supposed to be the most desirable thing I want to achieve. 

I want to be an English teacher in remote areas. I know my capabilities are still insufficient, but I hope to gain more knowledge and make much progress through distance learning and school courses. Before achieving my goal, I will work hard and enrich my teaching experiences and communication skills. 
（Translated by Lanis Wang)
