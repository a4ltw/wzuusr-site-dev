在柬埔寨，教育是奢侈?

溫暖白色巨塔的小螺絲釘團隊在0815與柬埔寨NGO Green Umbrella 創辦人MR Sokrath討論了未來可以合作事項。當中提到了柬埔寨因為學校師資的缺乏，造成學生上課時數縮減，原本有4小時的課，有些學校只有上2小時，有些甚至都沒有老師，因此常常「Free Time」。為了提升教育普及率及讓學習不中斷，以「Teacher-Trainer」的概念，Green Umbrella訓練了9年級生當英文老師，協助比他們年齡小的學生。Sokrath表示，這些9年級準英文老師們需要被裝備，他們必須知道”課堂管理”與”課程設計”，這立刻點燃了我們USR的服務精神因為這是我們的專業，老師們認真的在暑假進入討論以如何協助「Teacher-Trainer Program」。

On August 15, 2022, members of WHC-USR to participate in a video conference with the Founder of Green Umbrella- Mr. Sokrath on how to cooperate with in the future. During the meeting, it was mentioned that there is a lack of teachers in public schools in Cambodia, resulting in a reduction in the number of hours students attend classes. In some cases, some schools don't even have teachers so the students have “Free Time”. In order to improve the education, Green Umbrella uses the concept of "Teacher-Trainer", where 9th graders serve as English teachers to assist students younger than them. Sokrath said that these prospective 9th grade English teachers need know "classroom management" and "curriculum design", which immediately ignited the service spirit of our USR team because this is our profession. The teachers used their summer vacation to discuss how to assist the Teacher-Trainer Program.
