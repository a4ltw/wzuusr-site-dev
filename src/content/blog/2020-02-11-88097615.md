2020-02-10 by  季慕華 & 廖俊芳 (melody)
 
鋪路之旅-Paving the Way

(20200210旗山˙高雄) 為實踐文藻大學的社會責任，並儲備國際志工人才，文藻外語大學老師翁一珍、廖俊芳與季慕華一行，特別拜訪旗山國小，商談衛教英語體驗周末營的合作事宜。旗山國小是歷史悠久的百年名校，目前設有教育部英語村，教學資源豐富與環境優美，在該校李紋勝校長與呂昭伶教務主任的大力支持下，將簽署合作備忘錄，提供文藻師生服務學習場域，為偏鄉社區新住民子女提供特別設計的英語學習衛教課程。

(20200210 Cishan, Kaohsiung) To fulfill Wenzao’s university social responsibilities and to cultivate international volunteers, Wenzao USR delegates, including Prof. Janet Weng, Prof. Melody Liao, and Ms. Yolanda Chi, visited Cishan Elementary School, seeking cooperation on a weekend hygiene education English learning camp. Cishan Elementary School is a famous school with a history of over one hundred years, where an MOE English Village has been established with rich teaching resources in  the most beautiful environment. Strongly supported by Principal Wen-shen Li and Director Tsao-ling Lu, our USR team will soon sign an MOU, which will offer Wenzao faculty and students a service-learning site to provide especially designed English learning hygiene programs to children of new immigrants in the rural areas.
