WHC-USR第二場教師社群：
突破疫情封鎖，傳播愛到國際

天氣慢慢轉涼，國際疫情也日漸升高，如何突破疫情的封鎖，將台灣的專業及愛傳播到世界，已成為我們國際醫療志工服務的挑戰。因此，這次教師社群活動特別邀請醫界名嘴-台北榮總李偉強部主任來分享-「疫情下的國際醫療」，李偉強主任曾任國際醫療中心主任及衛生福利部醫事司司長，具有豐富的國際醫療經驗及視野，透過他的分享，相信一定能幫助我們震盪出更多國際服務學習的亮點！

The second lecture for faculty community: Break through the pandemic lockdown and spread love to the world

As it is getting colder, the alert for the Covid-19 pandemic is on the rise. How to break through the pandemic barriers and share professionalism and love with the world has become a challenge for our international medical volunteers. To this end, we have specially invited a medical celebrity--Dr. Wui-Chiang Lee, Director of Office of Medical Affairs Management, Taipei Veterans General Hospital--to give a speech on "International Medical Care under the Threat of COVID-19." Dr. Lee was formerly  the Director of International Medical Service Center, TVGH, and the Director of Department of Medical Affairs,  Ministry of Health and Welfare. Through his ample experience in international medical care and the grand vision shared in his talk, we will will be inspired in the sparkling ideas for international service learning.
