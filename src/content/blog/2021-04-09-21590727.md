狂賀小螺絲釘團隊榮獲2021第二屆《遠見雜誌》USR「楷模獎」
Joy over WHC-USR’s being granted “Model Award” in 2021 University Social Responsibility Awards by Global Views Monthly (By Yolanda & Melody)
 
   《遠見》高標評鑑USR 142件參賽方案，16座獎項出爐，獲獎率僅11.2%。狂賀文藻大學「溫暖白色巨塔的小螺絲釘——文藻國際志工共創就醫無障礙」以學校的外語優勢，鎖定地區新住民和移工就醫時語言溝通問題為主題，完整銜接學校特色， 榮獲福祉共生組「楷模獎」！
Adhering to very demanding criteria for evaluation, Global Views Monthly has selected only 16 out of the 142 projects, namely on average each with only 11.2% chance of winning the award. We share our joy with our friends from our base at Wenzao Ursuline University of Languages, where Wenzao Healthcare Communication-University Social Responsibility (WHC-USR) wields its advantage in foreign language ability to assist our targets--new immigrants and migrant workers--when they seek medical attention in local areas. Our team perfectly adopts the features of the university to fulfill the goals of creating welfare for co-living residents and is granted the Model Award.

   能夠入圍就真的已經很不容易，感謝主！現在又獲得「楷模獎」，真的是超乎我們所求所想！對我們這群歡喜心、甘願做的小螺絲釘們，真是超棒的鼓勵跟肯定！感恩天父再次恩待我們文藻這個美麗的花園！感謝所有WHC-USR園丁夥伴們，您們真的太棒、太優秀了，也辛苦大家了!
WHC-USR has been grateful for the chance of being nominated and now being honored by the Model Award is more than what we earlier expected. It is indeed a superb encouragement to everyone of us, who willingly dedicate time and efforts as “tiny screws”, as well as recognition of our work. We thank the Lord again for blessing the beautifully thriving Garden of Wenzao, and all the WHC-USR gardeners for their excellent collaboration.

   特別要感謝一路相伴，全力支持、相挺我們的合作夥伴們：高雄榮民總醫院、義大醫院、小港醫院、無著公益協會、Green Umbrella in Cambodia、One Forty、旗山國小、嶺口國小、左營國中、立德國中、瑞儀光電、得勝者協會以及黑橋牌公司。感恩有您們的扶持！讓我們能夠繼續為多元文化社會的共融、台灣年輕學子們的未來一起打拼，小螺絲釘團隊在此獻上我們深深的感謝！ 
  Especially we appreciate our partners for their companionship with full support throughout our service, including Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital, E-Da Hospital, Kaohsiung Municipal Siaogang Hospital, Asanga International Aid, Green Umbrella in Cambodia, One Forty, Cishan Elementary School, Linko Elementary School, Zuoying Junior High School, Lide Junior High School, Radiant Opto-Electronics Corporation, Champions Education Association, and Black Bridge Foods Co., Ltd. Without their help our work would not have been made possible. Let’s continue to work together for the harmony of a multi-cultural society and for the future of our next-generation learners. Here we extend our most sincere gratitude to all who stay with us!
