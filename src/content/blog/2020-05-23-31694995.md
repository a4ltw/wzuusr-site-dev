當USR與CSR碰撞-
USR防疫衛教志工與前鎮加工區越南移工同樂 
                               
2020/05/22 病毒不分國籍，防疫不分你我！
關懷因著語言及文化隔閡的移工族群，也為了杜絕可能的防疫缺口，文藻外大攜手長期投入社會公益、回饋地方的瑞儀光電共同舉辦越南移工-多語防疫衛教服務與宣導活動，共同為防疫盡一份心力！
   
今日活動由WHC-USR團隊的廖俊芳與梅馨云老師帶隊，協同19位衛教志工們不畏豪雨的來到瑞儀光電，現場有近30位熱情的移工們加入我們。 有別於以往一般的衛教上課，學生們設計了精彩且豐富的互動節目，配合中、英與越語的多語翻譯，透過歌謠律動、小短劇、有獎問答等方式，期盼能讓移工們從娛樂中可以學習到正確又實用的防疫觀念與知識。再次感謝瑞儀光電-張紋祥特助帶領著夥伴們與我們的結緣、相識與相惜！

Viruses are borderless, but epidemic protection is for everyone. It’s not the responsibility of doctors and nurses or even the patients but every of us should know the knowledge of hygiene, especially those workers who are from Southeast Asia working in a cultural different company in Taiwan. In order not to let them be the gap of epidemic, we held an epidemic prevention activity with Jui-I company who is also contributing a lot to the society.

The activity was directed by USR, Ms. Liao and Ms. Mei. Who took students to present dancing and singing related to epidemic prevention to those 29 migrant workers who were coming after work. Those  students presented something different from the general show in Chinese, English and Vietnamese through drama, singing and dancing, and QA games to promote the concept of epidemic prevention. We hope to let them form a good habit of a correct hygiene concept so that we all can back to a normal life and can work smoothly even prepare well for the future.
