2020-03-06 by Angie and Janny

微型課程與深碗課程開課介紹 

(2020-03-04 高雄文藻大學) 經過兩星期的等待，老師與學生們終於在三月三號及四號兩天相見歡了！這學期共有四位老師加入微型課程的教學，而深碗課程有11位老師各司專長的領域，將教導大家在醫療領導服務學習的理念與實踐！ 昨天晚上一開始由美樂蒂老師在幽默風趣又熱情的帶領之下，讓每位同學初步認識什麼是USR，做服務的社會責任有哪些，並闡述這門課的目標與理念。 前副校長陳立言老師則為大家介紹服務的精神與靈修為我們人生所帶來的影響。英文系翁一珍老師除了詳細的介紹說明整個課程的規定與要求外，也給同學們建立一個良好的學習與服務的態度，因為有良好的紀律，才有資格和能力去服務他人。英語中心季慕華老師是負責醫療翻譯的課程規劃，在她豐富的翻譯經驗中，相信能夠讓學生學得更踏實！英文系的老師周則銘昨天也與大家見面，未來他與王惠娟老師都會在翻譯小組帶領大家做醫療衛教翻譯的APP專業服務！教學組總召老師孫韵芳會統籌整個赴旗山國小做英語教學的服務與今年暑假到柬埔寨教學的計畫，相信有志於教學的同學已經迫不及待的想要加入了！外教系的梅馨云老師負責帶領學生為新移民與移工的服務，用衛教話劇與帶動唱和饒舌歌的表演方式，傳達目前疫情的衛教知識與因應方式！數位系陳泰良老師負責醫療衛教宣導影片的製作教學，讓我們整個USR的團隊更具科技應用的專業！ 這樣陣容堅強龐大的師資群實際投入這門課的教學，傾囊傳授知識與經驗培育專業服務領導的人才，真是文藻同學的福氣，未來希望這些學生也能將所學與服務的精神傳遞下去！

After two weeks of waiting, teachers and students finally met on March 3rd and March 4th. This semester there are four teachers joined Micro Course and the deep-bowl course has 11 teachers from different discipline to guide everyone through medical leadership and service learning theory and practices. Last night (3/4), the course begin with Teacher Melody guiding students to know what is USR, what are the social responsibilities in service learning and explained the goal and vision of the course with humor and enthusiasm. Followed by the ex Vice-President Mr. Chen Liyen introduced spirit of service and the influence of devotional practice on people.
During the second period, Teacher Janet Ong from English Department introduced the rules and requirement in detail and also developed good learning attitude with students, because it is necessary to have good discipline in order to service and help others. Teacher Yolanda Chi is responsible for the course plan on medical health translation. With her expertise, it is believed that students could learn a lot. Mr. Peter Chou from English Department also joined us last night, he and Teacher Hui-Chuan Wang will work together to lead students to work on APP in medical health translation.
Janny Sun will be responsible for the English teaching in both Chishan Elementary School on May 16-17 as well as the teaching in Cambodia in August. It is believed that students who are into teaching are eager to join.  Angie Mei from Department Foreign Language is responsible for the service learning in new immigrant and foreign workers, she will lead students to use role play, sing along and rap to teach the new immigrants and foreign workers knowledge of virus prevention.  Teacher Tailian Chen from Digital Content Application and Management Department is responsible for producing the promotion video on medical health. With such a strong teaching team devoted in this course, the students in Wenzao are truly blessed. We hope students who participated in this course could pass on what they have learned and the spirit of service learning in the future.
