終於迎來期待許久的USR深碗課程，首次上課有團隊8位老師廖俊芳教授、翁一珍教授、季慕華教授、孫韵芳教授、白豐碩教授、王慧娟教授、周則銘教授以及陳泰良教授，與計劃助理林思如、李世國和梁漢榮課程助理，接力介紹課程、國際志工說明以及USR團隊的官方網站等，為現場出席的46位學生更了解我們的計畫，並踴躍參與課程活動。

首先第一棒由廖俊芳教授以故事敘述方式講解課程與計畫緣起，並讓同學在短短20分鐘內認識團隊的菁英老師們，也讓學生在第一堂課以破冰方式讓現場活熱起來。第二棒則由翁一珍老師以簡潔並深刻的說詞，讓學生認真聽講在本課程中對於志工選擇、評量方法與課程安排。隨著來到第三棒由孫韵芳老師以生動、活潑的方式介紹柬埔寨國際志工宣導，以及上學期文藻國際志工在線上教學課程的成果影片，瞭解即使無法親自到柬國，文藻志工依然發光發亮，與合作伙伴一起教導柬埔寨小朋友英文。
    
而到課程的最後一棒，則交給說話搞笑中帶嚴謹的李世國助理介紹課程注意事項、排班規則；在切換身分後，搖身一變，則由兩位優秀的李世國同學以及蔡洛穎同學，介紹崇她社並邀請同學參加9/30傍晚的說明會。最後在今天快樂的歡笑與收穫滿滿的課程裡，拍照留下首次師生見面的回憶，期許學生在深碗課程中，可以學習專業，並透由志工服務實作來回饋社會，在他們的人生履歷表中留下最美好的經歷。

Here comes the first class of Deep Bowl Course! 8 professors: Prof. Melody Liao, Prof. Janet Weng, Prof. Yolanda Chi, Prof. Janny Sun, Prof. Albert Pai, Prof. Frances Wang, Prof. Peter Chou, and Prof. Toocool Chen, bringing project assistants Jollis Lin, Amos Lee, and Luong Han Vinh, are going to introduce the course, international volunteering opportunity, the website of USR team for 46 students who attend the class to have further understanding of our plan and join us! 

Our first speaker is Prof. Melody Liao, who is going to explain the course and the origin of this project and make students get to know the professional faculties on our team. Students can also spice up with icebreaker games. Our second speaker Prof. Janet Weng is going to introduce volunteering choices, evaluation methods, and course arrangement with simple and impressive speeches. Furthermore, our third speaker Prof. Janny Sun is going to introduce the international volunteering opportunity in Cambodia in a vivid way and also show the video of the outcome of online courses from last semester, understanding that although we could not be there teaching English, we are still working with each other. 

The last speaker is our witty and rigorous project assistant Amos Lee. He is going to announce notices, regulations, and class arrangement of the course. Next, two outstanding students, Amos Lee and Michelle Tsai are going to introduce Wenzao Golden - Z Club and invite all students to attend the club recruitment session in the evening on September 30th. Finally, at the end of today’s joyful and rewarding course, let’s take a picture to leave memories of the first meeting of faculties and students. We hope students are able to learn professions in the Deep Bowl Course, contribute to the society through volunteer services, and leave the best experience in their lives.
