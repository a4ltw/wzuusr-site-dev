by 林思如 趙立 季慕華

因爲疫情USR國際志工今年確認無法前往柬埔了。然因實在十分心繫村莊孩子們，希望藉由設計On Line課程以及影片拍攝來傳達來自台灣的愛。

現在人人手機不離手，拍照軟體也很多。基本的拍攝功夫幾乎人人都有，但要如何構圖、剪接及運鏡，是今天請到TVBS趙立攝影記者來分享給USR深碗課程-數位小組同學們的主因！

感恩趙記者專業、熱情及活力滿滿的現場授課，讓師生都感佩十分，收穫滿滿。期待數位小組同學們的好作品，讓我們USR線上課程內容更加精彩！

Because of the Covid-19 pandemic, our USR international volunteers will not be able to go to Cambodia this year. As we still care so much about the village kids, we hope to extend our love to them through designing an online course and making videos for them.
 
As people now can hardly live without their mobile phones and that there are many photo-editing software available, almost every one can easily apply basic skills but probably not such skills as composing, editing, or camera movement. Here with us today(2020/05/06) is Mr. Li Chou, a photographer from TVBS, who will share his expertise with our students of the digitalization group in our International Service Learning and Leadership course.
 
All teachers and students have been thrilled by Mr. Chou’s professionalism, passion, and vitality for work when he conducted his lecture. Enjoying a great harvest from a memorable lecture, we anticipate some good works from our students to enrich the USR online course.
