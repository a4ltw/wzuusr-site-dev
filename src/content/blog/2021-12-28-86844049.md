歡喜鄭勝峯副院長分享專業生涯規劃（by黃湘嫈)

鄭勝峯副院長開宗明義指出，他最重要的人生三部曲是
1. 熱愛工作；
2. 家庭為快樂泉源；
3. 一生堅守原則。

由此，他分享3個重點：工作（JOB）就是你每天的日常工作。可能會很辛苦，但能得到回報；職業（CAREER）就是一種增進自我成長進步的專業與技能；志業（CALLING）就是你用一輩子想去完成的。如果能同時成就這三項，就會是最完美的狀態!

鄭勝峯副院長分享其前往越南義診及演講的故事。他表示：「義診往往是短暫的，經常面臨到永續性的挑戰。」當他到越南義診時，因為能夠接受手術的孩童有限，「最令人傷心的是看到落選家長們失望的臉龐，這總讓他感覺內心過意不去。」所以鄭勝峯副院長總是萬分認真地把所有醫學知識傳授給當地醫生。因為越南是共產國家，學生需要政府同意才可以出國研修，難以得到較好的教育，所以鄭醫師盡力教導他們，準備Train the trainer 教學時，當地醫師們都非常認真的學習，這件事情也讓他覺得非常的欣慰。￼期盼他們能學到最好的醫療教育知識。

鄭勝峯副院長也分享了藉由提出雙向互惠的計畫，參與國際組織實在有非常多的好處。有如在越南義診，不僅可以提供團隊在實踐中有學習的機會，也能有跨文化的體驗。尤其在專業知識與技能，及跨團隊協作能力，都有相當的提昇。 透過跨國、跨文化彼此間的分工合作，分別在義診期間的前中後都需要許多緊密的溝通，以維持計畫順利運作，一同成長。最後副院長鼓勵我們找到自己的人生三部曲: 「讓人生多采多姿的辦法，就是看到機會就要去嘗試。」熱愛工作，找到自己的志業（志願）、把家庭當成是快樂泉源，並且終生堅守原則。他告訴大家，:現在就去做自己想做的事，不要等到以後、退休後再來追求自己渴望的。

團隊非常感謝鄭副院長分享其生涯發展與規劃，讓小螺絲釘們提前思索自己的未來志願，並更加了解現在我們所能做的是甚麼，提前累積資歷與經驗，才能在未來職場上無往不勝、精益求精!

 
Vice Director Sheng-Feng Jeng on Professional Career Planning Workshop

At the beginning of his presentation, Vice Director Shengfeng Jeng told us that the most important things in his life are: 
1. Love work
2. Family is the source of happiness
3. Stick to principles throughout your life
From this, there are three important points that he likes to share with us. First, JOB is your daily work every day. It could be very hard, but it can also be rewarding. Next, a CAREER is a kind of profession and skill to enhance self-growth and progress. Finally, your CALLING is what you want to do all your life. 

If people can achieve these three at the same time, they will be in the most perfect state! Vice Director Sheng-Feng Jeng brought us the story of his free clinic and speech in Vietnam. He said, "Free clinics are often short-lived and often face sustainability challenges." When he goes to Vietnam for free clinics and prepares for teaching, the local senior doctors will select medical students who are suitable for education, and the deputy dean will teach them the surgical knowledge. He said, "The saddest moment every time is to see the disappointed faces of the students when they were not chosen to receive the surgery. It always makes him feel sad." Therefore, Vice Director Jeng always tries to passes all medical knowledge to local medical students. Because Vietnam is a communist country, students need government approval to study abroad, and it is difficult for them to get a better education, so doctors try their best to teach them, hoping that they can learn the best medical education knowledge. 

Vice Director Jeng also shared the benefit of participating in international organizations by proposing a two-way reciprocal promotion plan. By providing free clinics in Vietnam, we are able to provide the volunteer clinic team the chance to learn from practice, provide cross-cultural experience, improve professional knowledge and skills, and enhance cross-team collaboration capabilities. Through the division of labor and close communication with each other before, during and after the clinic, we can keep the program running smoothly and grow with each other. In the end, the Vice Director Jeng encouraged us to find our own purpose of life: "The way to make life more colorful is to try when you see the opportunity." Love work, find your CALLING (volunteer), family as a source of happiness, and stick to principles throughout your life. He said, “Do what you want to do now, and don't postpone your longing until later or after retirement. Don't just do one thing because you’ll get tired soon.” The Vice Director brought us his career development and planning, allowing the students to think about their future in order to understand more about what we can do now, and with the accumulation of qualifications and experience, you will be able to excel in your future career!
