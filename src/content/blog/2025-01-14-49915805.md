🔥文藻USR × 芥菜種會：青春熱血，寒假首站直衝蚵寮！（撰文：林虹秀）
寒假的第一天，文藻USR團隊攜手從台北、新竹南下的芥菜種會夥伴，齊聚高雄蚵寮，開啟了一場熱血滿滿的半日參訪之旅！這次的任務很明確：與蚵寮國小和國中校長面對面交流，深入了解營隊場地的需求，並一起討論未來的合作模式。
參訪的第一站來到超有故事感的蚵寮國小！在校長的帶領下，團隊實地走訪了電腦教室、多功能教室、校史館等適合辦活動的場地，仔細記錄每個場地的條件與使用細節。校長還透露，希望透過這次合作，能讓學生接觸更多元、有趣的學習機會，讓大家都能玩中學、學中玩！
之後，團隊來到蚵寮國中，參觀學校的電腦教室和活動中心，並深入了解學生的學習需求與興趣點。校長特別提到當地學生對實用性和趣味性活動的高度期待，讓團隊有了不少靈感！文藻USR團隊與校方展開熱烈討論，構思如何結合資源，設計一個「學習×娛樂」的營隊課程。
當然，這次也少不了與芥菜種會的夥伴南下到場腦力激盪，討論營隊主題、教學資源配置以及志工協作等細節，大家的創意火花根本停不下來！
參訪在下午四點劃下完美句點，這次旅程不僅讓文藻USR團隊對場域需求有了深刻理解，也奠定了未來合作的紮實基礎。接下來，文藻與芥菜種會的夥伴們將攜手打造一場實體與線上並進的華/英語課程以及精彩無比的營隊，敬請期待！

Wenzao USR × Mustard Seed Mission: Kicking Off the Winter with Passion in Ke-Liao!
On the first day of winter break, the Wenzao USR team partnered with Mustard Seed Mission, traveling south from Taipei and Hsinchu to Ke-Liao, Kaohsiung, for an exciting half-day visit. The mission was clear: 
meet with the principals of Ke-Liao Elementary and Junior High Schools, explore potential event venues, and discuss future collaborations.
At Ke-Liao Elementary, the team toured various spaces, including the computer lab, multifunctional room, and school history museum, with the principal as their guide. Every detail about the facilities was recorded, and the principal expressed hopes for more diverse and fun learning opportunities for students.
Next, the team visited Ke-Liao  Junior High, where they learned about the students’ specific needs and interests. The principal emphasized the importance of creating practical and engaging activities, sparking numerous ideas for a “Learn and Play” themed camp.
The day also included a brainstorming session with Mustard Seed Mission, discussing camp themes, teaching resources, and volunteer collaboration. The team’s creativity was on fire!
By 4 PM, the visit concluded successfully. This journey not only deepened the USR team’s understanding of local needs but also set a solid foundation for future collaborations. Stay tuned as Wenzao and Mustard Seed join forces to deliver an unforgettable camp.
