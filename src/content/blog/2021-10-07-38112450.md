嶺口國小相見歡 （撰文/謝宇情)

日前(9/29星期三)文藻USR的老師和深碗課程的同學們，於線上一同參與和嶺口國小學生們的第一次相見歡。首先，由文藻同學們的自我介紹開始，為了勉勵小朋友們，有同學說「你不見得要很厲害才開始，但一定要先開始才會很厲害」，登高必自卑，行遠必自邇，大家對於學習一定要先下決心。

之後，大家各自登入了屬於自己的組別。團隊協同主持人周則銘老師還特別開車到嶺口國小現場協助小朋友們￼￼，才能讓文藻同學們開心的與嶺口小朋友們順利線上互動，透過閒聊的方式，加深彼此的了解和信任，也好讓之後的教學流程更順利。例如文藻大姐姐問小朋友們說「你們學校的英文課都在教什麼啊？」等問題，並向小朋友們說明將來的課將會以玩遊戲的方式來進行，上起課來會很好玩喔!小朋友也都充滿期待。

感謝一路陪伴與支持小螺絲釘團隊的葉鵲根校長才￼有了這次的相見歡，讓彼此不再陌生，並約定好下次的見面，開啟了英文學習的美妙旅程！

The meeting of Lingkou Elementary School

A few days ago (Wednesday, September 29), the teachers and the students of Wenzao USR participated in the first online meeting with Lingkou Elementary School students. The main purpose of the online meeting is for everyone to greet and get to know each other. First, Wenzao students introduced themselves one by one. Someone said, "You don't have to be very good to start, but you must start first to be very good", showing the determination to learn. 

After the introduction, everyone logged in to their own separate groups. The Wenzao students tried to interact with the Lingkou students. Through small chats, they got to know each other better so that the subsequent teaching process would be smoother. For example, the Wenzao students ask the children questions such as "What are you learning in English classes in your school?" and explain to the children that they will learn English by playing games in the future. The lessons will not be boring.

With this meeting, it let each students become more familiar with each other, they agree on the next meeting with each other, and start the journey of learning English!
