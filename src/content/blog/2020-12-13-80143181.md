你想突破自己嗎?想具備競爭能力嗎?想成為跨領域的人才嗎?
Want a breakthrough? Be more competitive? Aspire to become a cross-disciplinary talent?

「溫暖白色巨塔的小螺絲釘－文藻國際志工共創就醫無障礙」WHC-USR團隊總共規畫了4門選修課程！涵蓋9個系所中心、11位老師跨領域合作開設「國際專業服務領導」及「商管專業服務學習:醫療服務行銷與管理」。主題涵括：中、英語衛教營隊、跨文化與人際溝通、基本衛教與健康促進、繪本製作、數位化技能，以及期間的(國際) 志工專業服務學習實踐。2月22 -3月1日就是第二次加退選的時間，同學可要把握機會喔! 
WHC-USR Project is now offering a total of four elective courses in cross-disciplinary collaboration with 11 teachers from 9 departments and centers, under the titles of International Service Learning and Leadership (I), and Learning in Business Administration: Healthcare Marketing and Management (I). Scope covers English and Chinese Hygiene Education Camps, Cross-cultural and Interpersonal Relationship Communication, Fundamental Hygiene Education and Health Promotion, Picture Books Production, Digital Technology Application, and On-site (International) Volunteer Service Learning. Seize the chance to join us from Feb. 22 to Mar. 1st in your second round of elective course enrollment.
