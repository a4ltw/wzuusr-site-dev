醫護口譯組的同學有福了！
Blessed are those who are in the medical English interpreting  team! 

（高雄by 季慕華） 文藻USR團隊與口譯組教師，再度拜訪蔡淳娟博士，實際操作聯新亞洲醫學教育有限公司研發的模擬診斷病人系統。王慧娟老師與周則銘老師對該系統的設計感到非常興奮，並且在文藻翻譯所研究生洪同學，也是先前在義大醫院服務過的口譯志工的協助下，成功完成教學演練，並測試功能。老師隨即討論教學場景中的應用，如何在課堂上讓同學扮演不同角色，充分磨練口譯技巧。蔡博士也不斷提供醫學專業意見，和教學經驗，並樂意支持與大力配合文藻教學所需，嘉惠學子。文藻人有福了！
WHC-USR team and teachers of the interpreting group revisited Dr. Charity TC Tsai and operated the diagnostic simulation system developed by Landseed Asia Academy. Prof. Frances Wang and Prof. Peter Zhou were very excited about the design. With the help of Ms. Tinglian Hung, a Wenzao graduate student and formerly an interpreting volunteer at E-Da Hospital, a hands-on teaching demo and function testing was completed successfully. Teachers were immediately involved in detailed discussion on application to the teaching scenario, such as role-play and other ways to sharpen interpreting skills. Dr. Tsai provided numerous suggestions out of her medical expertise and teachings experience, gladly giving full support to Wenzao and addressing special teaching demands to benefit students. Blessed are the Wenzaorians!
