2020/05/17 by 季慕華
世界咖啡館，待續新樂章

WHC-USR 在旗山國小營隊，在英語衛教教學活動結束後，任務仍然持續。學生志工除了將場地復元外，垃圾也分類處理，以身體力行所宣導的觀念。隨後在翁一珍老師的帶領下，進行志工團隊的「世界咖啡館」交流討論，志工分享這兩天的活動心得，反思志工服務的意義，和連結個人生命的成長。這不是活動的結束，而是開啟人生新樂章。

2020/05/17 by Yolanda Chi

World Café - - a new chapter to be continued!
Right after the WHC-USR English Camp for hygien education at Cishan Elementary School, Wenzao students continued to accomplish their mission. Not only did they clean and restore the venues but they sorted and disposed the garbage as they had taught in the camp. Led by Prof. Janet Weng, a  "World Café" discussion was held as a followup for exchange of ideas. They shared feelings about the 2-day activity, reflected on the meanings of voluntourism, and linked to their personal growth. This is not the end of an event, but a new chapter in their life.
