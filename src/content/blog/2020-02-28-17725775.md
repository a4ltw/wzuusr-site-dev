by 季慕華

醫護教育與口譯的虛擬診間成真了!
A Virtual Clinic for Medical Education and Interpreting Has Come True!

(高雄 三民區) 因應疫情，這學期無法讓學生去醫院做直接的志工服務Direct Service，這個危機也是轉機，為了讓同學們能夠有機會做Indirect service 因此團隊老師們除了擬成立5種語文的官方Line帳號， 針對移工跟新住民需求，提供資訊，共同防疫之外，也苦思想如何加強同學們的臨床口譯能力。現在終於曙光出現，可藉著一套病人模擬系統，讓同學在安全無虞的環境下，吸收專業知識，實做口譯、跟讀Shadowing，體驗教育與業界的跨領域結合。
(Kaohsiung, Sanmin District) Due to prevention of Covid-19, our student volunteers are not allowed to offer “Direct Service” in the hospitals this semester, but this crisis has turned out to be a new opportunity. In order to provide the chance for “Indirect Service” we are planning to implement a 5-language official LINE account to assist new residents and migrants by providing the urgently needed information to fight the epidemic. In addition, we are seeking ways to enhance students’ clinical interpreting skills. Now a simulation system with virtual patients and clinic has dawned on us as the ideal way for students to absorb professional knowledge, practice interpreting and shadowing, and experience crossl-field collaboration between education and industry without any health hazards.

「溫暖白色巨塔的小螺絲釘─文藻國際志工共創就醫無障礙」六人行(廖俊芳教授、陳立言教授、翁一珍教授、陳泰良教授、季慕華老師、林思如助理)前往聯新亞洲醫學教育有限公司，拜訪創辦人蔡淳娟博士。蔡博士為高雄醫學大學前教務長，上學期曾來文藻為本計畫社群做專題演講，不僅在醫學界貢獻卓著，對醫學教育更是不遺餘力，近年來投入開發醫學診療知能互動學習軟體，在各知名醫學院系所與醫院深獲讚譽。由該公司系統管理與工程組曾名裕組長操作示範下，文藻團隊對醫護人員的教育和模擬醫療實務有了更深入的了解，此專業系統運用先進科技，內容豐富且具互動功能，亦可採用來訓練醫療口譯志工，提供最佳的擬真學習場域。
A group of six from Wenzao Healthcare Communication—USR (Prof. Melody Li, Prof. L.Y. Chen, Prof. Janet Weng, Prof. Tai-liang Chen, Ms. Mu-hua Chi and Ms. Jollis Lin) visited Dr. Charity TC Tsai, Founder of Landseed Asia Academy and also the former Dean of Academic Affairs, Kaohsiung Medical University, who came to give a lecture to our USR community last semester. Dr. Tsai has contributed greatly not only to the medical circle but also to medical education unreserved. Over the past few years, she has been dedicated to developing medical cognition interactive software, which has won praise and applause from distinguished medical universities, institutes, and hospitals. Following the demonstration of Mr. Ming Yu Tseng, Landseed’s Chief of System Management and Engineering, Wenzao teachers were able to gain insight into medical education and simulation of medical practice. With application of advanced technology, this professional system is rich in content and highly interactive. It can also serve for the training of medical volunteers on interpreting and translation, providing best learning sites on a virtual basis.
