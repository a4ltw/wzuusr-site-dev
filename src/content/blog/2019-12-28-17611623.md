各位同學們好，

「溫暖白色巨塔的小螺絲釘-文藻國際志工共創就醫無障礙」 USR 社會實踐計畫將邁入第3年，非常感謝同學們過去對USR團隊熱情地支持，參加我們跨域學習的課程。USR團隊在108下學期繼續規劃了4學分的深碗課程與1學分的微型課程(無學分超修限制)，可以深入體驗專業服務學習，並且邁向跨界、跨國合作互惠之路。課程目標期望能讓同學們擴大跨域視野、激發內在動力、強化轉移技能、體驗服務場域並且履行世界公民、擔任國際志工的好機會。期待有服務熱忱、願意離開舒適圈、挑戰自我的同學們，加入我們的團隊!!!
Dear Students,
Persuing the goal of “extending a helping hand in the hospital--creating a path to health care without language barriers with Wenzao international volunteers,” our USR Project is about to enter its third year and we appreciate your being our fervent supporters over the past years as well as participating in our cross-disciplinary courses. In the second semester of the 108 Academic Year, we offer a 4-credit so-called “deep-bowl” course and a 1-credit micro-learning course (not limited to the rule of maximum course credit cap), in which you will be able to deeply experience professional service learning and move toward cross-industrial and cross-national cooperation for mutual benefits. The course objectives aims at broadening your horizons, boosting your autonomy, enhancing your skills transfer, experiencing on-site service, and offering great opportunities for international volunteerism and fulfillment as global citizens. Those who are ardent for service and are willing to move out of their comfort zone for challenges are definitely welcome to join us.

🚩Course Name: International Service Learning and Leadership

為創造同學與醫療產業連結並擔任「醫療專業志工」之人生履歷機會，上述課程特別規劃「專業服務學習」場域，包括高雄榮民總醫院、義大醫院以及小港醫院及暑假柬埔寨🇰🇭（國際志工）
USR團隊敬邀
To establish links to the medical industry so as for our students to assume the roles of medical volunteers, our work sites for professional service learning in these two courses especially include Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital, E-Da Hospital, Kaohsiung Municipal Siaogang Hospital, and Cambodia (for international volunteers during summer vacation).
