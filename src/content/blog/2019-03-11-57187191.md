各位夥伴大家好
 
感謝過去一學期各位老師們熱情的參與與支持，是您們透過行動參與，啟動了這個按鈕，讓「文藻國際志工USR教師社群」得以圓滿成功，在此向您們致以最高敬意！ 
 
第一年進行的8場次教師社群活動，邀請校內3名講員及校外9名跨領域專家學者及業界代表蒞臨文藻，針對大學社會責任、專業服務學習、醫療專業服務與國際志工現況與需求等議題，於教師社群進行分享交流及深入探討，高達214人次參與USR社群活動，除了增進教師專業服務學習實務知能及對醫療專業領域之跨界學習，已成功的為本校教師與跨領域專家學者搭建起分享交流及深入溝通的平台，並且啟動與社區醫療機構洽談可能的跨域、跨專業合作。
 
本主持團隊秉持高品質的理念與熱忱，考慮到老師們因課程關係，無法每場參與，因此，我們蒐集老師們的意見，本學期教師社群時間將分別在週二跟週三來進行。其時段如下：

Dear all of you, 
 
Thanks to the enthusiastic participation and support of the teachers in the past semester, you have participated in this action and launched this button to make the "Wenzao International Volunteer USR Teacher Community" a complete success, and we would like to pay you the highest respect!
 
In the first year of the eight-time teacher community activities, three lecturers from the school and nine interdisciplinary experts, scholars and industry representatives from outside the school were invited to Wenzao for university social responsibility, professional service learning, medical professional services, international volunteer and other issue. We shared and had in-depth discussions in teacher community. Up to 214 people participated in the USR community activities. In addition to enhancing the professional knowledge of teachers' professional services and cross-border learning in the medical profession. They have successfully established a platform for teachers and interdisciplinary to share communication and in-depth communication. Moreover, they launched possible cross-domain and inter-disciplinary cooperation with community medical institutions.
 
The host team adheres to the high-quality concept and enthusiasm. Considering that the teachers are unable to participate in each session due to the curriculum, we collect the opinions of the teachers.
