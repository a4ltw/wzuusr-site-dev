(USR數位小編相見歡~)
日前(2020/03/24星期三中午12時)USR老師們召集每位數位小編於文藻校內聚餐，特別感謝USR的周則銘老師與陳泰良老師主動提供披薩以及胖老爹炸雞桶，用餐過程中，小編們透過自我介紹認識彼此（成員包括許甄芸Sharon、黃湘嫈Sandy、謝宇情 Winnie、邱珏綾 Jolin、楊靚紋 Jane、吳奕庭 Audrey、謝品安Nina），也許在未來有機會和彼此是合作夥伴，彼此一起互相學習、成長。
用餐之餘，老師們說明了小編們撰寫文章時須注意的一些事項與文章技巧，像是USR的英文系邱怡靜老師向小編們提出了假設性問題：若今天有個活動，成員的年齡層概括國中、高中和大學，應該如何做一個連結性的標題？如何創造出「梗」？如何寫出一篇具有吸引力的文章？然而，想吸引讀者觀看的訣竅是必須在文章中寫出人事時地物，才能清楚的表達事件的來龍去脈，另外，可以在文章中提倡USR計畫對於健康和教育品質的重要性(世界永續發展目標三、四)等等，這些都是小編記者撰寫文章的秘笈與方向；周則銘老師向小編們簡單說明USR計畫的主旨以及協助分配本學期各項活動的主題，其中的主題包括小港和義大醫院志工、國際志工、攝影展、USR演講協同教學以及協助校外營隊等等；陳泰良老師則是細心地介紹由他本人設計的高雄小港醫院專屬的Line自動回覆帳號，讓醫院的病人能有如此方便的設施可以使用，而USR的宗旨也正好是幫助社會上所需要幫助的人。
最後接近餐會尾聲時，老師們與小編們一同合照，以作紀念。同時，期許各個小編能在擔任職務中更加成長，也能撰寫出相當引人注目的相關報導。
 
(Meet the USR digital editor~)
A few days ago (12:00P.M on Wednesday, March 24, 2020), the USR Digital Editors held their first meeting at Wenzao University. Thanks to professors Peter Chou and Tailiang Chen who provided pizza and fried chicken buckets for the event. During the meal, the editors got to know each other through self-introduction (members include Sharon, Sandy, Winnie, Jolin, Jane, Audrey, and Nina). Maybe they will have the opportunity to be partners with each other in the future, learn from each other and grow together.
While eating, the teachers explained some matters and writing skills that editors should pay attention to when writing articles. For example, professor Jean Chiu from the English Department asked the editors a hypothetical question: If there is an event today and the participants’ age include junior high schools, high schools and universities, how should one make a cohesive title? How to create and write an attractive article? The trick to attract readers' attention is to write the 5Ws (Who, Where, When. What, and Why) in the article to clearly express the ins and outs of the event. In addition, the article can promote the importance of the USR plan to the quality of health and education. These are the secrets and directions of the editor’s reporters writing articles. Next, Professor Tze-Ming Chou briefly explained to the editors the various events of the USR project and assisted in the distribution of the various activities this semester. The events include volunteers training at Xiaogang and E-Da Hospital, Wenzao International Volunteers activities, photography exhibitions, USR guest speaker presentations, and assistance with off-campus camps. Finally, Professor Tailiang Chen carefully introduced the Line App exclusively for Kaohsiung Xiaogang Hospital designed by himself, allowing patients to conveniently find the facilities in the hospital. The purpose of USR is to help people in need of help in the society.
At the end of the meeting, the teachers and the editors took a group photo to commemorate the event. At the same time, it is hoped that the editors will grow even more in their positions and write quite compelling related reports.
