12/03WHC-USR教師社群活動報名開跑~

12月03日我們邀請到我們團隊長期的合作夥伴「無著公益服務協會」創辦人鄭南玉理事長及理事林彥婷醫師，向大家介紹柬埔寨服務村落的環境、生活與文化等跨國服務經驗分享。也即將會和我們在柬埔寨🇰🇭的好夥伴-Green Umbrella 線上連線喔！

無著公益服務協會創立於2014年，是目前台灣唯一於柬埔寨偏鄉進行長期扎根醫療教育工作的組織，尤其林醫師自2016年起即常駐柬埔寨。特別感謝無著公益服務協會提供給予我們的幫助與支持，讓我們的學生志工們能有機會連結國際服務場域！

(12/03) WHC-USR registration for faculty community events start!

On December 13th, we invited our long-term partners, Founder and Chairman Nan-Yu, Cheng and Director Yan-Ting, Lin, M.D., of Asanga International Aid to share with all participants their years of cross-national services in Cambodia, in addition to the introduction of the village environment, lifestyle, and culture.
Also, we will connect with our good friend partner in Cambodia-Green Umbrella.

Asanga International Aid was founded in 2014 and recognized as an NGO by Ministry of Foreign Affairs in 2015. Currently, it is also the only organization that lays groundwork and conducts long-term medical education in rural areas of Cambodia. Dr. Lin has been stationed in Cambodia since 2016. We especially thank Asanga International Aid for their help and support, which gives our student volunteers a great opportunity to serve internationally on the new horizons.
