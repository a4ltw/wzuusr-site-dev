遇見港醫  預見希望
In Contact with Siaogang Hospital, Encountering Hopes

［2020/08/06高雄，季慕華撰稿］文藻WHC-USR團隊一行10人拜訪高雄市立小港醫院夥伴，實地參觀學生志工服務場域，並討論新學期服務內容，和未來發展的可能。團隊抵達港醫後，首先在熙來攘往的群群病患中見到前來迎接的陳俊鴻國際長、社工室李庚恕主任、護理部蕭硯貞副護理長。目前在疫情仍然緊繃下，小港醫院全力滿足高雄南區對醫療的殷切需求，醫護人員功不可沒，也顯示志工服務極具意義。郭昭宏院長與洪志興副院長也特別於百忙中前來鼓舞士氣，更見小港醫院的領導力與動力。

會議由陳俊鴻主任開場，介紹新上任的護理部江秀珠主任。接下來是港醫簡報與志工工作分享，並播放小港醫院的創意宣傳短片。文藻團隊則由計劃總主持人廖俊芳教授一一介紹此次參訪成員，包括主持人吳甦樂教育中心陳立言教授、英文系翁一珍教授、數位系陳泰良教授、外語教學系孫韵芳教授、國事系主任賴文儀教授及英語教學中心副主任季慕華老師。同時還有大力支持本計畫的傳藝系蔡一峰主任、英文系白豐碩教授及法文系徐慧韻教授。廖俊芳教授說明回顧本計畫第一期的成果與第二期的發展重點後，由翁一珍教授說明109學年度課程規畫與志工的服務搭配，小港醫院亦提出未來可能增加的服務單位和規劃。雙方討論熱烈，達成共識，圓滿結束。

團隊也前往位於附近社區的娜麓灣文化健康站，此為小港醫院(高醫體系)與高雄市原民會合作設置的原住民都會區老人日間關懷站，推動日託、長照、衛教和文化傳承。文藻教師們實地了解醫院如何運用公私部門與社區在地資源協助都會原住民，落實複合式企業社會責任。期待藉由文藻USR+港醫CSR跨界、跨域合作中，雙方都能夠真正落實USR及CSR的真諦及願景，同時在追求「永續」發展的目標中更上層樓。

[2020/08/06 Kaohsiung, by Yolanda Chi] A delegation of 10 members from Wenzao Healthcare Communication—University Social Responsibility (WHC-USR) visited partners of Kaohsiung Municipal Siaogang Hospital in order to observe and experience at the work site of student volunteers, discuss the details of work in the coming semester and explore future possibilities. As soon as the team arrived, they were warmly welcomed by Dr. Chun-Hung Chen, Director Gung-su Li of Social Work Office and Leader Yen-Chen Hsiao of Department of Nurse, amidst crowds of patients in the hall, a sign that under the nerve-racking pandemic prevention period, KMSH endeavors to satisfy the urgent needs for medical care in southern Kaohsiung at its full capacity. The successful operation relies on every one of the medical personnel and underlines the significance of volunteerism. Superintendent Chao-Hung Kuo and Vice Superintendent Chih-Hsing Hung of KMSH especially came to offer support under their very tight schedules, revealing KMSH’s leadership and dynamic.  

Dr. Chun-Hung Chen started with an introduction of KMSH partners, especially Ms. Chiang, the new Director of Department of Nurse. Then there were a briefing made by the Public Relations, a presentation about the voluntary work, and a video with creative campaign in songs and dance. On Wenzao’s part, Project Investigator Prof. Melody Liao introduced the Wenzao delegates, including Vice Investigator Prof. Li-yen Chen, five assistant investigators (Prof. Janet Weng, Prof. Tai-Liang Chen, Prof. Janny Sun, and Prof. Mark Lai and Ms. Yolanda Chi), and Prof. Yi-feng Tsai, Prof. Albert Pai, and Prof. Elodie Hsu. Prof. Liao briefly reviewed the Project achievements in Phase I and the focal points of the development in Phase II, followed by Prof. Janet Weng’s illustration on the course design of the 2020 Academic Year and the links to voluntary services, while KMSH also proposed their plans for more units to be involved in the service. Both sides enjoyed a pleasant discussion and soon reached an agreement on the work this year, a perfecting ending for the meeting.

After the meeting, the Wenzao team moved to the neighboring Naruwan Cultural and Health Station, which is a day care center designed for the aboriginal senior citizens in the metropolitan area and sponsored by KMSH in collaboration with Kaohsiung City Indigenous People Committee to promote day care, long-term care, hygiene education, and cultural heritage. The Wenzao faculty learned on the site exactly how KMSH mobilized resources from the public and private sectors as well as community resources to assist indigenous people in the metropolitan areas to fulfill sustainable multifaceted corporate social responsibilities.
