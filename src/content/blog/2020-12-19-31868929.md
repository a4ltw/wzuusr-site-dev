愛的延續～2020柬埔寨🇰🇭線上教學反思 （蔡采妍同學撰文剪接）

還記得每次上課前，緊張、忐忑的心情，在看到小朋友們臉上大大的笑容後都一掃而空。看著鏡頭另一邊的他們，睜著水汪汪的大眼跟著我們複誦，回答問題時激動得幾乎用吼的，深怕我們會漏聽他們任何一個人的答案，課程的最後，我們手比著愛心和彼此說Thank you ,I love you！每次課程結束後，心靈都獲得無比的溫暖與快樂。國際志工師生團隊互相分享心得，互相鼓勵，一起期待在下次教學時變得更好！

法國人愛爾維修曾說過：「即使是普通孩子，只要教育得法，也會成為不平凡的人。」我們在有能力之餘，把手上的燈提高一點，照亮更多人。希望能透過教育，在小朋友的心中種下一個個小小的種子，期待有一天能開出一朵朵不凡的美麗花朵！

2020 Cambodia International Volunteer online teaching video Written by Ivy Tsai
2020/12/19

Still remembered the feeling of nervousness before each class, all that feeling disappeared when we see the big smile from the kids. Looking at them through Zoom, hearing them repeating and shouting the answers out loud, this is what we do every Wednesday at 15:30. Knowing that the kids in Cambodia are
 looking forward to our English lesson, we feeling warm and happy at the end of each class when we say Thank you, I love you to each other. As volunteers, this is what kept us going.

As Helvetius, Claude Adrien once said: “With adequate education, even an ordinary child could become extraordinary.” If we hold our hands higher, we could lighten more people. We hope we could plant a little seed in kids’ mind through education, hoping that one day a beautiful flower may bloom.
