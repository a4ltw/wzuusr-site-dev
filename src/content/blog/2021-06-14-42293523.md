英文課輔期末線上見面會(撰文/謝宇情)

日前(110/6/2) 10位WHC-USR立德國中英文課輔服務團隊與立德國中被輔導的學生，以及師長們(文藻翁一珍老師、周則銘老師、廖俊芳老師，立德洪瑞鍇校長、丁韋安老師，得勝者教育協會吳繡娟主任)一同出席英文課輔期末線上見面會，彼此分享對於參與本課輔計畫的感想。

在服務的期間，課輔志工們與學生們除了一起玩遊戲、交換資訊，也一起享受製作母親節卡片的樂趣。到了五月中旬，不幸的是，因疫情肆虐只好將所有課程改為線上教學，但即便如此，仍然澆不熄彼此的熱情，「只要抱持著熱忱，就可以讓世界變得更好」翁一珍老師說道。

接著，由幾位老師分享對於本次計畫的心得，文藻廖俊芳老師說：「這次服務方案成功的關鍵在於『合作』，並且需要雙方都有熱忱，才可以建立互惠多贏的服夥伴關係。」；立德洪瑞鍇校長則說：「我看到了不一樣的教學方式，印象最深刻的是，有次因段考的關係必須停課一次，看到了孩子們失落的神情反而有點高興，代表他們對這堂課有所期望。」

同時，文藻課輔志工們也闡述著自己的感想，有人說：「看著認真學習的孩子們，就如同投射出自己的影子，只是現在換自己擔任輔導孩子們的志工，希望他們能打開視野、快樂學習。」，也有人對孩子們說：「我們的出現，就像種下一顆種子，總有一天，將會發芽成長，成為你們生命中特別的過客。」

對立德學生們來說，這次的計畫不只是學習英文怎麼拼音、拆音節，最重要的是看見大哥哥及大姐姐們的專業服務熱忱，期盼此大手牽小手的￼服務方案，能夠為雙方的學生們灑下成為世界公民的種子。￼￼

A Final Celebration and Reflection of the After-school English Tutoring Project
By Winnie Hsieh

A few days ago (110/6/2), 10 student volunteers from WHC-USR project met with students from Lide Middle School along with teachers (Professors Janet Weng, Peter Chou, Melody Liao of Wenzao; Principal Ruikai Hong and teacher WeiAn Ding of Lide Middle School; and Director Xiujuan Wu of the Winners Education Association) attended the online meeting to celebrate the end of the After-school English Tutoring Project and shared their thoughts about participating in this project. 

The project started on March 31, 2021 when the volunteers, students and the staff from Winners Education Association met for the first time. It was also the beginning of the After-school English Tutoring Project. In addition to playing games and exchanging information with each other, they also enjoy the fun of making Mother's Day cards together. By mid-May, unfortunately, due to the raging epidemic, all the courses had to be changed to online teaching, but even so, the enthusiasm of each other could not be quenched. "As long as you hold on to the enthusiasm, you can make the world a better place" said Professor Janet Weng.

Then, several teachers shared their experience of this project. Professor Melody Liao said, "The key to the success of this class assistance is cooperation, and it requires enthusiasm from both sides to be able to learn eternally." Principal Ruikai Hong also added, "I have seen a different teaching method. What impressed me most was that there was a time when classes had to be suspended due to a period of examination. I was a little happy to see the children’s lost expressions, which means that they were very happy about learning from the Wenzao students and they have expectations about this class. "

The Wenzao student volunteers then shared their feelings. One student said, “Looking at the children who study hard is like projecting from their own experience, but now they are changing themselves as volunteers to tutor the children. I hope they can open their horizons and learn happily.” Another student said, “Our appearance is like planting a seed. One day, we will sprout and grow and become a special visitor in your life.”

For the Wenzao students who volunteered their professional services at Lide Midddle School, this project is not only about learning how to teach English, but learning through service. Only by maintaining enthusiasm can be enthusiastic and help those who really need help.
