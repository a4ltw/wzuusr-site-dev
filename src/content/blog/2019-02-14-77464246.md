2019 柬埔寨國際志工第二次招募(Final Call)

一、計畫說明: 
「溫暖白色巨塔的小螺絲釘－文藻國際志工共創就醫無障礙」USR社會實踐計畫，從文藻出發，結合鄰近文藻之醫療院所資源及跨校跨領域合作，並充份發揮文藻外語能力暨國際化的優勢，推動多國語國際醫療志工專業服務、創作多國語故事繪本及文宣品，以及執行第二年的國際志工服務，希冀共創「醫療無國界，就醫無障礙」的願景。本計畫藉由正式課程、非正式課程，以及專業服務，帶領文藻師生與社區/國際組織合作，以增進專業實務及職能，同時落實大學社會責任，增進學生服務社會的價值觀，培育世界公民的國際視野。
“The Tiny Screws of the White Tower – Wenzao International Volunteers for Borderless Medical Care Access” is a Wenzao-sponsored USR program that seeks to combine the bountiful resources of local medical institutions, with the multifaceted and specialized skillset of students at Wenzao University. The goal is to create an interscholastic platform that makes full use of the students’ broad spectrum of language skills and abilities.  These students will then be given the unique opportunity to try their hand in the multidisciplinary world of international volunteer medical services.  They will work to create an array of multilingual storybooks and texts, and will be instrumental in the implementation of the volunteer program’s second year.  The hope is to help achieve the program’s overall vision of “medical care that is both accessible, and without borders”.

預計2019年8月中上旬 (實際日期待確認後公布) 
The plan is expected to go into effect by mid-August (The actual date will be announced after final confirmation). 
三、服務地點: Service Location
柬埔寨茶膠省村莊 
Takéo Province in the southwest of Cambodia.
四、招募對象: Recruitment Targets
本校專科部4年級、大學部1-3年級學生，且具備良好的溝通表達、環境適應、團隊合作能力 
The program is looking for people who are either in their 4th year of Five-Year Junior College Program, or in their 1th-3rd year of Four-Year Bachelor Program.  Good communication skills, ability to adapt to new environments, and team working skills are highly recommended.   
五、費用 Cost
1.旅費包括來回機票、住宿、膳食、交通、文化探索(金邊、暹粒、吳哥窟等地)
Overall costs include round-trip airfare, accommodations, meals, transportation, as well as cultural expeditions to surrounding destinations such as Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, Angkor Wat, etc. 
2.參與學生須自行負擔1萬元整，超過1萬元的部分將由學校及教育部USR補助
Students participating in the volunteer program will be required to pay a flat fee of 10,000.  Any further remaining expenditure will be covered by the school, as well as the USR program itself. 
五、國際志工任務: International Volunteer Tasks
1.協助醫療團隊當地村莊衛生教育宣導工作
Assist local medical teams to help promote health education in the village.
2.執行當地村莊學校衛生/環保教育主題營隊活動
Work with village schools to create camp activities revolving around health and environmental education.
3.執行當地村莊學校課後語言輔導課程活動
Partner with village schools to host after-school tutoring sessions.
