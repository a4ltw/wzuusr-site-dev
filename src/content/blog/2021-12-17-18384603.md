1102小螺絲釘USR課程開跑囉~

非常感謝過去4年，曾有583位同學參加我們跨域、跨界的USR專業服務學習課程，充分展現文藻人的利他行動力! 邀請您來參與小螺絲釘團隊，一起用生命熱情來幫助、支持我們所看見世界及台灣社會的需要。

WHC-USR團隊在110-2繼續規劃了1門「4學分的深碗課程」 (代號1283) 與2門「1學分的微型課程」 (代號1287;1288) ，都是讓同學有機會連結醫療產業、提昇跨域競爭能力及履行社會實踐的好機會。課程除了讓同學有機會選擇擔任醫療志工外，也特別規劃兒童醫院說故事志工、英語教案製作與教學技巧，運用所學到中小學舉辦健康促進英語營隊或進行偏鄉線上遠距英語教學服務，同時也歡迎參與Cambodia 及Vietnam國際志工服務（場域之選項會考量疫情)，讓我們一起邁向世界公民之路！

 Over the past four years, 583 students have participated in our international cross-disciplinarily professional service-learning courses. These students demonstrated the spirit of Wenzao with their benevolence to assist and support those in need in Taiwan and around the world.

For 110-2 academic semester, the Wenzao Healthcare Communication USR project is offering one 4-credit “deep-bowl” courses (course number 1283) and two 1-credit micro courses (course number 1287; 1288). These courses offer a good opportunity for you to connect with the medical industry while fulfilling social responsibility.   

In addition to the opportunity to serve as general volunteers in the hospital, opportunities for professional volunteer services include: storytelling in hospitals, basic health education and health promotion, participation in camp activities, picture book making, digital skills and international volunteer service learning (the selection of service areas will be based on the development of the epidemic). Let’s go on the road to world citizenship together.
