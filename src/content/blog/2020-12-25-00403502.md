(01/06) WHC-USR registration for faculty community events start!

WHC-USR will invite Dr. Nail-ching Chen, a neurologist at Chang Gung Hospital and also the Executive Chief of Kaohsiung Dementia Care Center, for a lecture at the USR teachers’ community. As Taiwan has become an aging society, dementia are now the urgent challenges confronted by numerous families as well as the unignorable concerns over our own wellbeing. Dr. Chen has years of experience in care and treatment of dementia and will be able to bring to us new perspectives and decoding of the dementia myths. As the message Dr. Chen would like to convey to patients and their families, and to us teachers as well, “no one was born with all capabilities, but we may learn together, work together, and grow older and wiser together.”(handshake)(handshake)
