CSI的熱情與願景-高峻凱醫師的分享。撰文：武玉康 (國際志工)。編輯：李世國

 今晚(11月24號)的深碗課程，我們很榮幸能夠邀請高峻凱醫師來到文藻與我們分享有關「臺灣兒童健康暨身心發展協會」（CSI Taiwan）這個非營利組織在做的事情。在演講中，我們瞭解了CSI的核心理念，追循三個主要的國際指標：世界人權宣言、兒童權利公約和永續發展目標。 同時我們也瞭解了CSI TAIWAN 在SDGs中相對應的目標，其中包括SDGs -2(終止飢餓)、-3(良好健康與社會福利)、-4(優質教育)、-6(清潔飲水與衛生設施)和第17項(全球夥伴關係)，以及他們在實踐過程中是如何實現這些目標的。 

尤其我們深受CSI為柬埔寨村莊孩童的付出而感動。為柬埔寨兒童提供豆漿蛋白質營養，以及為他們提供牙科護理（SDGs 3），建造圖書館、提供繪本、規劃共讀等（SDGs 4），以及為村民提供水的過濾及倡導煮沸（SDGs 6）等等。 憑藉著無私奉獻精神和努力，使得飢餓人數減少和兒童健康狀況的大幅改善，如此取得輝煌的結果也就不足為奇了。
至於我的想法，我深深對CSI臺灣能創出這麼多驚人壯舉感到驚訝和印象深刻。 不僅如此，他們願意在幫助改善兒童健康方面的奉獻精神和實際付出及努力讓我深深的感動。 他們所做的工作已在國內和國際上產生了超棒的正向影響。

Today, we are honored to be able to invite Dr. Kao Jun-Kai to come to Wenzao and give a speech about CSI Taiwan- a Taiwan-NGO organization focused on improving global children’s physical and mental health.
During the speech, we were introduced to the belief of CSI, which comprises 3 main ideas: the universal declaration of human rights, the convention on the rights of children and the sustainable development goals. Later on, we were introduced to the CSI SDGS goal which comprises SDGS2,3,4,6,17 and how they have executed these goals in real life. Furthermore, we got to know about CSI activities in Cambodia and how they execute these SDGSs. For example, provide soybean milk for nutrition to Cambodian children and also dental care for them (SDGs 3), build library and provide books for Cambodian students (SDGSs 4) as well as giving water filtration to Cambodian villagers( SDGs6) and so much more. With that much dedication and effort, no surprise that the results are brilliant with the number of starvations reduced and Cambodian children's health improving dramatically throughout the year.
As for my thoughts, I am really astonished and impressed by so many amazing feats that CSI Taiwan has achieved. Not only that, but I was also impressed by the dedication and effort they are willing to put into their work in helping to improve children's health. The work they are doing not only have a huge impact to the society, nationally and internationally, but also draw a lot of attention from many organizations and people to join in, give a hand to help not only poor children in undeveloped country like Cambodia but also poor children from any corner of the world, so that we can elevate the overall health being of everyone.
