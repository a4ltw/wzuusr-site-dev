你想突破自己嗎?想具備競爭能力嗎?想成為跨領域的人才嗎?
Want a breakthrough? Be more competitive? Aspire to become a cross-disciplinary talent?

WHC-USR團隊總共規畫了4門選修課程！涵蓋9個系所中心、11位老師跨領域合作開設「國際專業服務領導」及「商管專業服務學習:醫療服務行銷與管理」。主題涵括：OSCE國考/標準病患培訓及認證、醫療口譯技巧、健康促進、繪本製作、數位化技能以及跨醫療產業的服務學習實踐。9月13 -21日就是第二次加退選的時間，同學可要把握機會喔! 

WHC-USR Project is now offering a total of four elective courses in cross-disciplinary collaboration with 11 teachers from 9 departments and centers, under the titles of International Service Learning and Leadership (II), and Learning in Business Administration: Healthcare Marketing and Management (II). Scope covers English and Chinese Hygiene Education Camp, Standard Patient training and certified , Medical Chinese-English interpretation, Cross-cultural and Interpersonal Relationship Communication, Fundamental Hygiene Education and Health Promotion, Creating Picture Books , Digital Technology Application, and On-site Hospital Service Learning. Seize the chance to join us from Sept. 13-21 in your second round of elective course enrollment.
