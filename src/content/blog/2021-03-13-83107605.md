03/25 WHC-USR教師社群活動報名開跑~

    感謝各校師長在疫情期間共體時艱，落實教育及防疫工作。新學期開始，小螺絲釘老師們將持續邁向教育目標和願景，並深耕本土文化，以培養孩子接軌國際視野。
    本學期第一場教師社群，邀請到國中、小學校長們，舉辦一場座談會，大家坐下來聊聊教育目標和教學理念。在21世紀一切多變的世代，國中及小學要如何面對這樣多樣化的環境，同時讓孩子有愉快的學習生涯？在高雄面對不同的學生族群，以及多元化的教育環境，需要因材施教以激發學生學習熱情。結合國內外多方資源，營造國際全球視野，樹立優良典範，讓學生建立良好目標並往前邁進。從小學到大學，不論是10歲或20歲的教育路程，都值得一起交流分享，期待您一起參與，讓座談會碰撞出不同的火花，實踐創新教學方式。

We are truly grateful for those faculty who put such great efforts on education and disease prevention during the trying time of the Covid 19 pandemic.The new semester has begun and we expect to continue to move towards our education goals and vision and enhance connections to the international society by cultivating our young learners locally. 

In the first session of our teachers’ community, we will invite a few principals from elementary and junior high schools in Kaohsiung for a panel discussion on education goals and teaching approaches. Confronting changes in the 21st Century, how should schools at all levels adjust to the varied scenarios and provide an enjoyable learning experience? In Kaohsiung, as challenges come in dealing with students’ different ethnic groups and diverse cultures in the teaching environment, it will be necessary for us to arouse students’ passion for learning in accordance with their capabilities and interests. We intend to integrate resources at home and abroad to nurture a global perspective and establish a model of discipline for students to follow in their tracks. Whether you would like to target on elementary school or university education, or aim at the development of the (one)(zero)-year-olds or the (two)(two)-year-olds, you are welcome to share with all the participants. Let’s make this discussion a booming exchange that will inspire and initiate action for innovative teaching approaches.
