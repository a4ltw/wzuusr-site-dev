USR團隊11位老師合開一門深碗課程，其中有14位已甄選上今年的暑假柬埔寨國際志工！全部同學們也都已安排分別到三個醫院（高榮、義大及小港醫院）擔任醫療志工！
每每看到這些孩子們的學習熱情❤️，再累也值得了！祈願我們自己的孩子也都能夠碰到有教學熱情的老師。

Regarding the “Deep Bowl Course” opened by 11 teachers of our USR team, among them we have selected 14 students for this summer's international volunteers in Cambodia.

All the students have also arranged for medical volunteers in three hospitals (Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital, E-Da Hospital and Kaohsiung Municipal Siaogang Hospital)!

Every time we see the enthusiasm of these children, it’s worth it! Pray that our own children will also be able to meet teachers with enthusiasm for teaching.
