(20201010 By 崇她社社長 蔡洛穎)
哈囉各位同學👋
崇她青年社在10/6（二）晚間舉辦的招募說明會已圓滿成功囉🙏
 
崇她青年社💡，英文名Zonta，源自美國原住民語—誠實，與值得信賴。
誠如先前宣傳文，崇她青年社會利用各項資源替女權及弱勢發聲，而遍布全台及海外的崇她母社與社姐們，將提供龐大的資源讓青年社參與各項大專院校、社區及國際性服務計畫。
 
崇她青年社上學期的社課內容主要是讓同學們認識崇她、認識女權，團體討論中學習批判性思考，除此之外，也替弱勢國家孩童義賣籌措資金，亦同時讓同學們踏出實現社團理念的第一步！👍🏿👍👍🏻
 
希望大家可以盡心享受在社團當中，大聲說出你們想改變的不公，伸出手握住你們看見需要幫助的那顆心，無論今天你是什麼樣的角色，只要你有想法、有服務熱忱、符合崇她青年社社團理念，都歡迎你著手開始實現！☀️☀️
 
(20201010 By Zonta club leader Michelle)
Hi, everyone! The recruitment orientation of Wenzao Zonta club was rounded off this Tuesday. Zonta, a word derived from Indigenous languages of the Americas, means "honest" and "trustworthy".

As aforementioned, Zonta club is dedicated in empowering women and the underprivileged worldwide through service and advocacy. Volunteer opportunities and resources are provided here in Zonta club, helping you to make contributions.

In this semester, we plan to help all the members get a better understanding of Zonta club and feminism with different activities. We also hope that everyone can improve critical thinking skills through the education sessions. Besides, we will organize a charity sale to raise donation for the elementary school students in Cambodia. Such activities are all designed to help members to put their philosophies into practice.

We welcome students who share the same ideas, who have passions in volunteer work, and who want to make changes in the future! Register now and come join us!
