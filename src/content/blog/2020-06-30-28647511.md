傳愛到柬埔寨。

by 翁一珍 & 季慕華

五月份深碗課程口罩教學組同學以及旗山國小學生共同完成的環保口罩套及祝福卡片，經過遙遠曲折的海上旅程，已經安全抵達柬埔寨，當地的小朋友們及老師們收到時都非常高興，創辦人Sokrath表示，接收到的不只是新的防疫物資，更感受到來自台灣文藻同學們滿滿的愛心。

Love, extending in Cambodia

The mask covers and the greeting cards made by all volunteer teachers and local Taiwanese children at the Cishan Healthcare Education English Camp in May safely arrived in Cambodia after a long and winding journey. The Cambodian children and teachers were glad to receive not only the newest type of materials for epidemic prevention but also the overflowing love from Wenzao University in Taiwan, the one that will extend continually in Cambodia.

By Janet and Yolanda
